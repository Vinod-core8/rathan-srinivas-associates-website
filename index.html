A Short Guide

How to Choose the Right Advocate in Bengaluru

A few things worth checking before you commit to any lawyer, in this city or elsewhere.

Check Practice Area Fit

A divorce matter, a property dispute and a criminal case call for different experience. Choose an advocate who regularly handles your type of matter.

Verify Court Experience

Ask whether the advocate personally appears before the Karnataka High Court, City Civil Court, Family Court or Magistrate Court — not just handles filings.

Ask for a Clear Strategy

A good advocate should lay out likely remedies, timelines, risks and documentation needed, once they understand the facts of your matter.

Get Clarity on Fees

Fees vary by case complexity, consultation time and urgency. Ask upfront, and get it in writing before proceeding.

Look for Clear Communication

You should know who to reach and how, especially when a matter is time-sensitive — not wait days for an update.

Prefer Direct Access

Ask whether you'll be dealing with the advocate directly through the matter, or handed between associates as it proceeds.
